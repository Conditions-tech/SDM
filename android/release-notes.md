# SDM for Android 1.0.0

The first Android version of SDM (Smart Download Manager): free and open source (Apache-2.0),
built from the same design and safety rules as SDM for Windows.

## What it does

- **Fast downloads:** up to 8 connections per file, pause and resume (also after closing the
  app), a queue (3 at a time), and a history. Finished files go to **Download/SDM**.
- **Share → SDM:** in Chrome or any app, tap Share on a link and choose "Download with SDM".
- **Converter:** turn a finished download into MP4 or M4A (no quality loss) or MP3.
- **Video pages (optional, off by default):** turn on in Settings to save public videos from
  YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and other sites as 720p, 1080p, MP3 or M4A. You are responsible
  for following each site's terms. SDM never uses your accounts, cookies or logins, and can't
  download DRM-protected or private videos.
- **Safe by design:** no downloads from your phone or your Wi-Fi's devices, safe file names,
  nothing overwritten, and SDM never opens or installs what it downloads.

## Install

1. Download `SDM-android-1.0.0.apk` below on your phone.
2. Open it. Android asks to allow installs from your browser or Files app: allow it once.
3. Needs **Android 10 or newer** on a **64-bit** phone (nearly all phones from the last 6 years).
