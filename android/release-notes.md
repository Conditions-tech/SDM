# SDM for Android 1.1.0

SDM (Smart Download Manager) for Android: free and open source (Apache-2.0). This version adds
a music player, a video player and English subtitles to the downloader.

## New in 1.1.0

- **Music player:** all the songs on your phone (after you allow access) by songs, albums,
  artists and folders, with search. Shuffle, repeat, a queue, playlists, playing in the
  background with lock-screen and notification controls, and a **sleep timer** (15 to 90
  minutes, your own time, or the end of the song, with a gentle fade-out).
- **Video player:** full screen; swipe for brightness (left), volume (right) and seeking
  (sideways); double-tap to skip 10 seconds; speed 0.5x to 2x; a touch lock; it remembers
  where you stopped.
- **English subtitles:** subtitles inside the movie file are offered, and English is chosen by
  itself. Load your own .srt or .vtt file, or find English subtitles on OpenSubtitles.com (add
  your own free API key in Settings). Out of sync? Move them 0.5 s earlier or later.
  Video-page downloads also save the page's English subtitles where the site allows it
  (YouTube currently doesn't).
- **YouTube MP3s keep the original language:** SDM picks the video's original sound, never a
  dubbed track.
- **A new dark design**, led by album art.

## What it does (as in 1.0.0)

- **Fast downloads:** up to 8 connections per file, pause and resume (also after closing the
  app), a queue (3 at a time), and a history. Finished files go to **Download/SDM**.
- **Share → SDM:** in Chrome or any app, tap Share on a link and choose "Download with SDM".
- **Converter:** turn a finished download into MP4 or M4A (no quality loss) or MP3.
- **Video pages (optional, off by default):** turn on in Settings to save public videos from
  YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and other sites as 720p, 1080p, MP3 or M4A. You are responsible
  for following each site's terms. SDM never uses your accounts, cookies or logins, and can't
  download DRM-protected or private videos.
- **Safe by design:** no downloads from your phone or your Wi-Fi's devices, safe file names,
  nothing overwritten, and SDM never opens or installs what it downloads (it only plays music
  and videos in its own players).

## Install or update

1. Download `SDM-android-1.1.0.apk` below on your phone.
2. Open it. Android asks to allow installs from your browser or Files app: allow it once.
   Updating from 1.0.0 keeps your download history.
3. Needs **Android 10 or newer** on a **64-bit** phone (nearly all phones from the last 6 years).
